About SimpliFi

Portfolio look-through and educational research — not investment advice.

SimpliFi is a web application for tracking portfolios, looking through ETFs to the underlying holdings, comparing funds, scanning dividends, and putting inflation in context. An AI-assisted Investment Explorer is included for educational research — not personalized recommendations.
