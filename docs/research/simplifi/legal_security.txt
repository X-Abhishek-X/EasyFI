Security

How we approach protecting your account and data.

Last updated October 19, 2026

This page summarizes how SimpliFi approaches security today. It is product documentation for users, not a formal audit report.

What we do not store

We do not store brokerage or bank login credentials. Portfolio holdings are entered by you in the app, or — where brokerage linking is enabled — read from your brokerage through SnapTrade. When you link a brokerage, you sign in on SnapTrade's Connection Portal; your brokerage password is never sent to SimpliFi. The connection is read-only: SimpliFi cannot place trades, move money, or change anything at your brokerage.

We store a per-user SnapTrade access secret, your brokerage account names with masked account numbers, and the positions and cash balances we import.

We do not ask for Social Security numbers or similar government identifiers as part of the core product.

Authentication

Deployed environments use Clerk for sign-in. Account security also depends on you: use a strong unique password (or SSO), and protect your email account. Enable MFA in Clerk when available. Payments are processed by Stripe; we do not store full card numbers.

Transport and hosting
Deployed app and API traffic use HTTPS.
Application data is hosted on Amazon Web Services. The primary database runs in a private subnet, reachable only from application services — not from the public internet.
The database uses storage encryption at rest (AWS RDS). That encrypts the disk volume. We do not claim application-level (field-by-field) encryption of portfolio rows.
Secrets and environment configuration for deployed environments are stored in AWS Secrets Manager, not checked into source control.
Unlink versus delete

You can unlink a brokerage at any time from Settings → Linked brokerages or a portfolio's Holdings page. We ask SnapTrade to delete that connection. Imported positions stay in your portfolio as holdings you can edit and stop updating from the brokerage.

Cancelling a paid subscription does not unlink a brokerage or delete your account. Permanent deletion is a separate request — see Privacy and Refund and Cancellation.

Application practices
Portfolio and user data are accessed through authenticated API routes scoped to the signed-in user.
Sensitive fields (authorization headers, secrets) are redacted from application logs where configured.
Audit logging may record security-relevant actions (for example account or portfolio changes) for operational review.
Market data

End-of-day prices and related market data come from third-party providers (for example Tiingo). That data is market data, not your brokerage credentials. Accuracy and latency limits are described in our site disclaimer and Terms.

Incident notice

If we become aware of a security breach of our systems that affects data retrieved through SnapTrade, we notify SnapTrade within 24 hours, as required by their developer terms. We will also contact affected users when we are required to, or when it would help them protect their accounts.

Limitations

No internet service or storage system is 100% secure. Despite commercially reasonable efforts, we cannot guarantee absolute security. If you discover a vulnerability, please report it to customer.support@simplifinow.co.
