Billing FAQ
What am I buying?
One plan unlocks the full SimpliFi app: portfolio look-through, compare, Investment Explorer, dividends, inflation context, and EOD valuations. There is no free tier of those tools.
Does the subscription auto-renew?
Yes. Monthly and annual plans renew automatically until you cancel. You can cancel anytime in Settings → Manage billing.
How do I cancel or get a refund?
Cancel in Settings → Manage billing. Cancellation stops future renewals. You keep access until the end of the period you already paid for. Your login and portfolios stay, so you can subscribe again later. Cancelling does not unlink a brokerage or delete your account. To permanently delete your account and the data we hold, email customer.support@simplifinow.co. There is no delete button in the app. A verified request removes the login, the SnapTrade user and brokerage connections, the access secret we store, and the portfolio data we hold for the account. See our Refund and Cancellation Policy for when we refund a charge.
Taxes?
Prices are shown in USD. Applicable tax may be added at checkout based on your location.
